Every adult deserves a real home.

Affinity Home Care is a Colorado Program Approved Service Agency (PASA). We match adults with intellectual and developmental disabilities with host home families, and we stay involved for as long as the placement lasts.

What we stand for

Three commitments behind every placement.

Licensed and compliant

We follow Colorado's PASA and Medicaid waiver requirements, including background checks and home reviews.

Careful matching

We match on routines, needs, and personality, so the home fits the person.

Ongoing support

Coordinators check in with individuals, families, and providers after move-in.

Who we support

Adults with intellectual and developmental disabilities.

Many of the people we support also have medical, behavioral, or mental health needs. We match them with providers who have the experience and training for those needs.

Have a question for our team?

Families, case managers, and future providers are all welcome to reach out.
