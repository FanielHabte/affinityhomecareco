About Us

Who We Are

A Colorado-licensed host home agency committed to safe, dignified, community-integrated care for individuals with developmental disabilities.

"Our mission is to build lasting relationships between individuals with developmental disabilities and qualified host home providers — creating real homes, not programs."

Our Story

Community care, done with intention

Affinity Home Care Co. was founded on a simple belief: every person deserves to live in a real home, surrounded by people who genuinely care. As a licensed Provider Agency (PASA), we operate under Colorado HCPF, ensuring every placement meets rigorous safety and compliance standards.

🏥
