About Us

Who We Are

A Colorado-licensed host home agency committed to safe, dignified, community-integrated care for individuals with developmental disabilities.

"Our mission is to build lasting, caring relationships between individuals with developmental disabilities and qualified host home providers — creating real homes, not programs."

Our Story

Community care, done with intention

Affinity Home Care Co. was founded on a simple belief: every person deserves to live in a real home, surrounded by people who genuinely care for them. We saw too many individuals with developmental disabilities placed in settings that felt institutional — so we built an agency focused entirely on quality host home placements across Colorado.

As a licensed Provider Agency for Supported Living Services (PASA), we operate under Colorado's Department of Health Care Policy and Financing (HCPF), ensuring every placement meets rigorous safety, care, and compliance standards.

Our Values

What guides every decision we make

🛡️
Safety First

Every home undergoes a thorough assessment. Every provider passes background screening. We hold safety as non-negotiable.

🤝
Dignified Care

We treat every individual as a full person — not a case number. Our placements center autonomy, preference, and quality of life.

🌱
Community Integration

We believe people thrive when they're part of a community. Host homes keep individuals connected, active, and included.

📋
Compliance & Accountability

We operate fully within Colorado HCPF regulations and maintain transparent reporting standards for all placements.

💬
Open Communication

Families, case managers, and providers stay informed. No surprises — clear updates at every stage of the placement journey.

🎓
Provider Development

We invest in our host home providers with ongoing training, resources, and direct agency support so they can deliver excellent care.
