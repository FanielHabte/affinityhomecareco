Find the Right Home for Your Loved One

Safe, community-based placements for individuals with developmental disabilities — fully covered through Colorado Medicaid waiver programs.

What is a Host Home?

A host home is a private residence where a trained, licensed provider shares their home and daily life with an individual with a developmental disability — an alternative to group homes or institutional settings where the individual is treated as part of the family.

Who Is Eligible?

  • ✓
    Diagnosed developmental disability
  • ✓
    Enrolled in a Colorado Medicaid waiver (SLS, DD, CMHS, or CES)
  • ✓
    Assessed need for community-based residential support
  • ✓
    Referred by a case manager or self-referred with waiver documentation
