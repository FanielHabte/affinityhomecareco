Who we support

Is host home living a fit?

  • An adult (18+) with an intellectual or developmental disability
  • Enrolled in, or applying for, a Colorado Medicaid waiver
  • Needs support with daily living
  • Would do well in a family-style home instead of a facility

Not sure about the waiver? We'll work it out with you and your case manager.

What happens next

Five steps from first call to moving in.

    We check in regularly and stay involved.

Common questions

What families ask us first.

What is a host home?

A private family home where an adult with developmental disabilities lives with a trained provider who supports them every day. It's an alternative to group homes and facilities.

How is it paid for?

Care is funded through Colorado Medicaid waivers. We'll walk you through what's covered, including any room-and-board costs, during your consultation.

Can our family stay involved?

Yes. Families and guardians are part of planning, and visits and time together are encouraged.

What if the match isn't right?

We stay in touch after move-in. If a placement isn't working, we plan a respectful change to another home without a gap in care.
