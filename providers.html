The process

Apply

Submit your application by phone or online form.

Background Screening

State and federal checks for all adults in the home.

Home Assessment

We verify your home meets Colorado safety standards.

Training

Complete required certifications — provided at no cost.

Matching

We match you with a compatible individual based on your household and preferences.

Ongoing Support

Continuous check-ins, resources, and 24/7 on-call access after placement.

Basic requirements

Age 21 or older

Required for all primary providers.

Clear background check

All adults in the household must pass state and federal screening.

Safe, accessible home

Must meet Colorado safety standards and include private space for the individual.

Commitment to training

Required certifications are provided by us — you just complete them.
