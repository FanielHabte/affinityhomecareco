How to Get Started

The path to becoming a host home provider

The 6-step process

Apply Online or by Phone

Submit your application and tell us about your home, household, and motivation for becoming a provider.

Background Screening

Required criminal background checks for all adults in the home — state and federal. Handled by our team.

Home Assessment

We visit your home to verify that it meets Colorado safety and accessibility standards for host home care.

Training & Certification

Complete required training on care techniques, medication management, incident reporting, and individual rights.

Matching

We carefully match you with an individual based on compatibility, support needs, location, and household dynamics.

Placement & Ongoing Support

Once placed, our team supports you continuously — with check-ins, resources, and 24/7 on-call access.

Basic requirements

🪪
Age 21 or older

All primary providers must be at least 21 years of age.

🔍
Clear background check

State and federal criminal background screening required for all adults in the household.

🏠
Safe, accessible home

Your home must meet Colorado safety standards and provide private space for the individual.

📚
Willingness to complete training

Required certifications are provided by us — you just need to participate and complete them.

❤️
Genuine compassion

The most important qualification: a sincere desire to support and include another person in your home.
