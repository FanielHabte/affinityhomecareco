Become a host home provider

Open your home. Change someone's life.

Host home providers share their home with an adult with developmental disabilities and are paid through Medicaid for the support they give. We train you and stay with you.

What it takes

The basics.

  • 21 years or older
  • Pass a background check
  • A safe home with a private bedroom
  • Complete the required training
  • Commit to ongoing support and check-ins

How to become a provider

Six steps from application to welcome home.

  1. Apply

    Tell us about you, your household, and your home.

  2. Background check

    Standard checks for adults in the household.

  3. Home visit

    We visit to make sure the home is safe and suitable.

  4. Training

    Required training, scheduled with you.

  5. Matching

    We introduce you to someone who fits your home.

  6. Ongoing support

    A coordinator stays with you after move-in.

Ready to open your home?

The application takes a few minutes. A coordinator will reach out to talk it through.
