Host Home Placement

✓ Medicaid Funded

Our core service. We match individuals with developmental disabilities to carefully screened, trained host home providers who offer a safe, family-style living environment in the community.

  • Thorough compatibility matching process
  • 24/7 on-call agency support
  • Regular home monitoring visits
  • Individualized Support Plan (ISP) compliance
  • Transition support from prior placement

Personal Assistance Services

✓ Medicaid Funded

Skilled support for activities of daily living (ADLs) — helping individuals maintain independence, dignity, and meaningful daily routines within their host home or community.

  • Personal hygiene & grooming support
  • Meal preparation & nutrition guidance
  • Mobility and transportation assistance
  • Medication management support
  • Community participation and outings
Learn More
🌿

Respite Care

✓ Medicaid Funded

Planned or emergency relief for primary caregivers and host home providers. We ensure continuity of care while the primary caregiver takes needed time to rest and recharge.

  • Short-term and extended respite options
  • Planned or emergency scheduling
  • Vetted, trained respite providers
  • Seamless handover documentation
  • Available statewide across Colorado
