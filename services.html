Services

Support built around a home.

Three services, all funded through Colorado Medicaid waivers. Most people start with host home placement.

Our core service

Host Home Placement

An adult lives in a provider's family home, with daily support for personal care, health, and community life.

For adults who want a family-style home instead of a group setting.

PAS

Personal Assistance Services

One-on-one help with everyday tasks like hygiene, errands, and getting around.

For adults who need hands-on help to stay independent.

Short-term

Respite Care

Temporary care in a trusted home, so routines continue while a caregiver rests.

For families and host home providers who need a break.
